NATIVE COMPUTER

Exact-window computer control

Start Local Computer Helper to enable desktop control.

Two capture modes: Observe takes one exact-window snapshot. Share keeps a persistent operating-system exact-window stream open. Supported input compares the platform's foreground/window-focus oracle, active desktop, and physical pointer before and after delivery and fails if those samples differ; matching samples cannot rule out a shorter transient change. Both modes use your shared login session, not a VM or separate desktop.
