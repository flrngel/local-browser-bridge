NATIVE COMPUTER

Exact-window computer control

Start Local Computer Helper to enable desktop control.

Two capture modes: Observe takes one exact-window snapshot. Share keeps a persistent operating-system exact-window stream open. Supported input seals an exact-target route and samples the platform's foreground/window-focus oracle, active desktop, and shared-pointer boundary before and after delivery. An unknown route or boundary fails closed; concurrent pointer activity is reported as contaminated without attributing it to a physical device or the helper. Matching samples still cannot rule out a shorter transient change. On macOS, focus-capable input may briefly make the exact target AXFrontmost under a restored focus lease without raising it or switching WindowServer's OS-front process/window. Both modes use your shared login session, not a VM or separate desktop.

Persistent exact-window share is inactive.
